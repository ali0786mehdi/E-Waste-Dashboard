## ⭐ Featured: Maharashtra E-Waste Tracker

> **Can Maharashtra's recycling infrastructure keep up with the waste it generates?**

A district-level study of **10 districts**: **78,300 MT** of e-waste generated, against **80 collection centres** and **65 authorized recyclers** (FY 2024–25).

**What it found:** infrastructure follows Tier-1 city generation, led by Mumbai and Pune, while developing districts such as Kolhapur and Amravati need stronger awareness drives.

| Deliverable | What you get |
|-------------|--------------|
| 📄 `report.html` | Academic report: executive summary, district analysis, findings, recommendations |
| 📊 `dashboard.html` | Interactive dark-themed Chart.js dashboard: generation vs. recycler capacity, with status badges |

**Data source:** `Maharashtra_EWaste_Dashboard_Dataset.xlsx`
