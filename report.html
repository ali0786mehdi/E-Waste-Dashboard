Key Findings & Recommendations

  • Strong Correlation: The dataset reveals a near-perfect correlation between e-waste generation volumes and the deployment of infrastructure (collection centres and recyclers). Mumbai City leads all metrics significantly.
  • Tier 1 Dominance: Mumbai (City + Suburban), Pune, and Thane collectively account for 70.3% (55,100 MT) of the tracked generation, and correspondingly host the vast majority of collection and recycling resources.
  • The Developing Regions: Kolhapur, Solapur, and Amravati are classified as "Developing". While generation is lower, the density of awareness drives (only 1 per district) suggests a need for proactive educational campaigns to prevent informal sector expansion in these areas.
  • Recommendation: Scale up awareness drives in Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities (Aurangabad, Nashik, Kolhapur) to drive volume towards the formal collection centers before generation scales exponentially.
